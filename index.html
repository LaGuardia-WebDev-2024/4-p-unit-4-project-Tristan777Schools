My Unofficial Local Guide to Angel Island

Carnival Night Zone

Let's take a trip down to the carnival! Best to visit this place during the nighttime so you can see this area's beautiful lights illuminating the dark sky. Shoot yourself out of a cannon, jump off of popping balloons, cling onto rotating wheels and visit the BARREL OF DOOM!! With tons of fun to have, you'll find yourself having a jam!



IceCap Zone

Ever wanted to go snowboarding? There's no better place than the fabled IceCap Zone! Look for the exit cannon in Carnival Night, hop in, and go flying in the air to end up in this little winter wonderland, where you can immediately begin the action by grabbing that board and shooting down the hill. Explore the glistening caves and watch out for any falling icicles!



Sky Sanctuary Zone

Looks like we're really high up in the air! Sky Sanctuary is exactly what it sounds like. A sanctuary in the sky! Take a walk and explore the ancient structures that float high above the already floating island. Bounce on clouds and spin around on the rotating plate to get to other platforms. Hang onto vines and make sure you run fast enough any crumbling bridges. Be careful, don't fall!
